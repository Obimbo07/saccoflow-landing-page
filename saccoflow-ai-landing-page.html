Why SACCOs hit a wall before they hit a market

The core workflows — onboarding, origination, risk, disbursement, repayment, support — are standard. The policy on top of them never is. That's why generic banking software gets rejected and paper survives.

01

Problem

Manual loan approvals, member onboarding and reporting mean slow turnaround, inconsistent risk calls, and no real analytics — usually run by one overworked loans officer.

02

Solution

AI-assisted risk assessment, automated KYC onboarding, live financial dashboards and a member chatbot, covering everything from application to disbursement with a full audit trail.

03

Outcome

SACCOs scale membership without scaling headcount, and officers work off a scored, ranked queue instead of a stack of paper files.

5 days → 4 hours  ·  +45% satisfaction

How a loan actually moves through Saccoflow

One path, from a member's first message to money in their account.

01

Member applies

Through whichever channel the SACCO already uses — WhatsApp, USSD, or the web portal. No new habit to learn.

02

Automated KYC

ID and mobile-money history are checked automatically, before the application reaches a human.

03

Risk scoring

A model trained on SACCO-specific default patterns, augmented with the transactional data the SACCO already holds, scores the application.

04

Officer reviews a ranked queue

Not a stack of paper — a queue sorted by risk, with the reasoning behind every score. The officer still decides and approves.

05

Disbursement over M-Pesa

Funds move the moment an officer approves. No separate banking step, no manual entry.

06

Repayment and support, automated

Scheduling, reminders and grace periods run themselves. A member chatbot handles balances, statements and top-ups without a human touch.

The moat isn't the software. It's the intelligence layer.
