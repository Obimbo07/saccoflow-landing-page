You keep your system. We make it easier to use.

Your SACCO already has a system that keeps member accounts, savings and loans. We don't ask you to throw it away. We sit on top of it and give your members and staff a much simpler way to get things done.

01

What you have

A system holding your members' savings, loans and records, and the rules your SACCO has agreed on.

02

What we add

An app for your members, simpler tools for your staff, and AI that helps both of them get answers and finish tasks.

03

What doesn't change

Your records, your loan rules, and who approves what. We don't take over any of that.

We connect to what you already run

Every SACCO's setup is different. We look at the system you use today, work out how to connect to it, and handle that part with you and your system provider. You don't need to change anything on your side to get started.

Once we're connected, what your members see in the app comes straight from your own system — their savings, their loans, their balances. Nothing is made up and nothing is kept separately.

If something can't be connected, we tell you early and plainly, instead of promising it anyway.

AI that sits on top of all of it

Not a chatbot bolted on the side. The AI runs through the whole app — guiding members around it, answering their money questions, helping them plan, and giving your staff a hand with the heavy lifting.

Members don't have to hunt through menus. They say what they want and the AI takes them there, fills in what it already knows, and walks them through the rest. For members who aren't comfortable with apps, this is the difference between using the SACCO and giving up on it.

Most members never get financial advice. The copilot looks at their own savings and loans and helps them think it through — what they can afford, what a loan would cost them each month, how to clear it faster, and what saving a bit more would do. It explains, it doesn't push.

How money moves

Money moves the way it already does for your SACCO — through mobile money and your bank. We just make it happen from the app, and make sure it ends up recorded in your system.

When a member deposits, repays or withdraws, we start the payment, follow it through, and show the member and your office the same status at the same time.

We're not a bank and we don't hold your members' money. Your SACCO's system stays the record of who has what.
